# Introduction to Smart Materials: Bridging Mechanics, Polymers, and Advanced Engineering

"Smart materials" have rapidly evolved into one of the most exciting and widely applied interdisciplinary fields in modern engineering. These unique polymeric and non-polymeric materials exhibit unusual—and often optimized—behavior and engineering properties in response to external environmental stimuli. Consequently, leading researchers across mechanical engineering, polymer science, materials science, and chemistry are actively engaged in studying and advancing this frontier technology. 🔬⚙️

In collaboration with the esteemed [**FaraDars**](https://faradars.org/) platform, I am thrilled to have prepared an introductory course designed to explore these materials, their key characteristics, and their rapidly growing applications. 📚✨

I hope this tutorial serves as a valuable and practical starting point for students, researchers, and anyone passionate about advanced and smart materials.
