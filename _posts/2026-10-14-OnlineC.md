### 🌟 Exploring the Fascinating World of "Smart Materials"


«Smart Materials» have rapidly evolved into one of the most exciting and dynamic interdisciplinary fields in both academia and industry today. 

Their defining characteristic is the ability to exhibit dramatic responses to environmental stimuli—displaying unusual, tailorable engineering behaviors and properties. The modeling and fabrication of smart materials span across various branches of mechanical engineering, polymer science, materials science, and chemistry. 🔬⚙️

In collaboration with [**FaraDars**](https://faradars.org/), I have prepared an introductory course designed to familiarize you with these materials, their key characteristics, and their growing applications. This tutorial serves as a useful and practical starting point for students, researchers, and anyone passionate about advanced and smart materials.

### 📌 Course Outline & Key Topics:

* **Introduction & Fundamentals:**
  * Introduction to smart polymeric materials
  * Key characteristics of smart materials
  * Various stimuli and responses of smart materials

* **Advanced Material Classes:**
  * Hydrogels
  * Dielectric Elastomer Actuators ($\text{DEAs}$)
  * Liquid Crystal Elastomers ($\text{LCEs}$)
  * Shape Memory Polymers ($\text{SMPs}$)

* **Applications:**
  * Soft Robotics
  * Smart Structures
  * Biomedical Applications
  * Wearable Electronics

* **Evaluation:**
  * Advantages, challenges, and limitations of soft materials
